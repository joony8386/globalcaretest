About Global Care

A Thailand-based provider of medical products and solutions—specializing in the import and export of devices and equipment for high-specialization surgery, with timely attention and a human approach to partnership.

Mission

What guides our work

Empowering Thai healthcare professionals through the distribution of premium surgical products and equipment for everyone’s needs.

We work to improve and prolong people’s lives by pairing high-quality products with tailored services for the clinicians and institutions who rely on them every day.

Scope of business

What we do

Our services span private sales, clinical education and support, and government e-bidding—so hospitals and surgical teams can evaluate, adopt, and sustain the tools they need.

Private sales

Implants, general instruments, specialized equipment, and single-use consumables across disciplines such as orthopedics, cardiology, and hospital planning.

Sales support

Education for healthcare professionals on product usage—from foundational surgical technique training to advanced on-site solutions—delivered by an experienced, certified team.

E-bidding

Hands-on experience with Thailand’s government e-bidding system, including legal standards and procedures, plus support teams that help manage bidding efficiently.

Our values

How we show up

Six principles shape how we partner with manufacturers and healthcare professionals—and how we support patient care behind every case.

Reliability

Ensure consistent availability of essential medical products and equipment so providers can meet clinical demand with confidence.

Unity

Foster strong partnerships with manufacturers and healthcare professionals to strengthen industry-wide cooperation.

Innovation

Embrace advances in healthcare technology and practice to continually improve what we offer and how we serve.

Empathy

Recognize the impact on patients’ lives and approach every interaction with compassion and understanding.

Excellence

Strive for the highest quality in products and services to support superior patient care.

Adaptation

Stay agile in responding to changing industry needs, trends, and market dynamics.

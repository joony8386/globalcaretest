Process

Start with a short brief

You don’t need a finished protocol. A one-page outline of aims, setting, timeline, and what kind of support you’re exploring is enough for a first meeting.

  1. Send a brief via the contact form (choose “Research proposal”).
  2. We schedule a discussion—virtual or in the Lab.
  3. Together we clarify scope, feasibility, and next documentation if any.

Note: Interest does not imply funding or formal sponsorship. Each proposal is reviewed case by case.
